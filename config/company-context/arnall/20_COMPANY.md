# Arnall company context

## Verified public facts

- Arnall presents itself as **Arnall Carniceros & Xarcuteros**.
- Its official website lists six establishments: Palamós, Platja d'Aro, Palafrugell, S'Agaró, Sant Antoni de Calonge and Torroella de Montgrí.
- The official offering includes meat from Girona-area farmers, in-house specialties, cured meats and cheeses, prepared food, wines, cava and selected artisan products.
- Public industry coverage describes Arnall as a family business founded with its first butcher shop in Palamós in 1972 and led by the second generation since the 1990s.
- Public reporting in 2025 identifies an alliance with Ametller Origen to jointly operate its butcher-shop business. Treat operational details of that alliance as time-sensitive and verify them before use.
