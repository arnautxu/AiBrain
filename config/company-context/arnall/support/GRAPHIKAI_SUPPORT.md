# GraphikAI support

GraphikAI maintains the reusable AiBrain product and the versioned installation/release path used for Arnall. Arnall remains a separate installation with its own context, identities, secrets and durable data.

## Current support boundary

- Product configuration, provisioning guidance and release evidence are versioned in the AiBrain repository.
- No support note grants access to Arnall data or authorizes an external mutation.
- Backend CI, GHCR publication, deployment and authenticated live acceptance are separate states.

Named support contacts, service levels, incident channel, maintenance window and escalation policy are **unknown** until supplied and approved.
