# Arnall company brief

Use this skill only inside the Arnall installation.

Read `resources/confirmed-context.md` before making Arnall-specific claims. Treat operational details as unknown unless a current authorized Arnall source confirms them. Current prices, hours, locations, people, agreements, and news are time-sensitive and must be verified before use.

Never invent stock, suppliers, recipes, financial data, employee responsibilities, schedules, or internal procedures. Keep public facts distinct from internal facts and preserve source attribution.
