# Review the final artifact

1. Check exact slide count, required content and format, factual claims, sources, units and independently recomputed totals against the brief.
2. Render every page from the latest draft with aibrain_documents.render. Record the source SHA-256 and reviewed page numbers in private QA notes. Rendering a page without inspecting its returned image is not review.
3. Inspect every image: clipping, text wrapping (especially numbers with units), contrast, footnote size, logos, chart labels, axis meaning, table completeness, diagram connectors, overlap and margins. Compare with the source for template work.
4. Read all titles and body copy together. Remove vague slogans, unsupported causal claims, redundant subtitles and formulaic lists. Use precise statements or topic labels. Confirm language and company voice.
5. Inspect deck-level pacing and composition. Avoid repeated UI cards and identical chart-plus-callout layouts unless justified by a comparison or template. Do not add decoration solely for variety. Ask whether the audience can identify each slide's main point immediately.
6. Repair the authoring source in one batch. Re-export and review every page of the final source; the previous hash does not validate changed bytes. Do not loop indefinitely on cosmetic choices. Unresolved material defects block a claim of finished work.
7. Deliver the requested PDF and/or PPTX from the same reviewed source through the authorized delivery tool. A delivery receipt proves persistence, not visual quality. Never invent a finalizer receipt or claim software checks that were not executed.
